CHUD 0.5.0

Questie support. If Questie is installed, CHUD picks up its quest objectives, turn-ins and quest givers and shows them on the bar with Questie's own icons. It switches on by itself and only reads from Questie, it never changes anything there. If one of Questie's own settings is hiding something, the matching CHUD option greys out and its tooltip tells you what to turn on.

Focused quest. Your focused quest gets a bigger icon (it never makes the bar taller) and its other objectives still show as normal icons. Click any smaller objective on the bar to focus that quest. There's a Focused Quest Only option, and the focused icon can glow while you're standing in the quest area.

Less clutter. Only the nearest icon of each objective type per quest is shown. Objectives and quest givers shrink and fade the farther away they are, with their own Fade Distance sliders (default 300 yd). Questie Max Distance defaults to 600 yd and goes up to 1000.

Smoother icons. Nearby icons used to jump four times a second. They now update up to 12 times a second while you move, and there's an Icon Update Rate slider if you want to trade smoothness for CPU.

New options. Icons Outside Bar puts the icons just past the edge of the bar. A button at the bottom of the settings page shows or hides the default XP bar.

Themes. Two new themes, Catppuccin and Tokyo Night. A new Daylight checkbox next to the theme list changes the colors with the in-game clock every half hour and locks the color controls while it's on.

Performance. The marker code uses roughly a third of the CPU it did before.

Settings menu. Everything in Display and Experience Bar, plus all the buttons, now has a tooltip.

Your 0.3.0 settings carry over, so the new default distances only apply if you reset them.
