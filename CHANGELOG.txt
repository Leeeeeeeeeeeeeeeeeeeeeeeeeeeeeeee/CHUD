CHUD 0.4.0

Questie support
CHUD now reads Questie's data and shows its icons on the compass. It turns on by itself when Questie is installed and never changes anything in Questie. Quest objectives, turn-ins and quest givers use Questie's own art, so a loot objective shows as a bag and a kill objective as a sword. If Questie hides something in its own options, the matching CHUD option greys out and its tooltip tells you which Questie setting to turn on.

Focused quest
Your focused quest gets one larger marker, and every other objective of that quest still shows as a normal icon. The large marker never makes the bar taller. Clicking any smaller quest objective on the bar makes that quest the focused one. The new Focused Quest Only option hides every other quest's Questie icons. Standing inside the quest area can make the focused marker glow, using the same quest area the game's own quest helper uses (Glow In Quest Area, shown only if the client supports it).

Distance and clutter control
Questie Max Distance (default 600 yd, up to 1000 yd) sets how far away Questie icons appear. Only the nearest icon of each objective type per quest is shown. Quest objectives and quest givers now shrink and fade with distance. Objective Fade Distance and Giver Fade Distance both default to 300 yd. Smaller When Far still applies to the other marker types.

Smoother icons
Icons within about 150 yd used to jump four times a second. They now update up to 30 times a second while you move, and only the icons are redrawn. The new Icon Update Rate slider (4 to 60 per second) lets you trade smoothness for CPU. Standing still costs nothing extra.

Icon placement
New option Icons Outside Bar puts the marker icons just beyond the bar's edge, below it when the bar is in the upper half of the screen and above it otherwise.

Themes
Two new built-in themes, Catppuccin and Tokyo Night, use the colors of two popular editor palettes. A new Daylight checkbox next to the theme list makes the colors follow the game's clock every half hour. While it is on, every color control is greyed out and the box reads Daylight On: Colors Locked. Turning it off brings your previous colors back.

Settings menu
Every option in the Display and Experience Bar sections, the background and marker style choices and all the buttons now have tooltips. Clicking a checkbox's label now works as well as clicking the box. A new button at the bottom of the settings page shows or hides the default game XP bar. Quest Objectives and Turn-Ins is on by default.

Notes
Settings you saved in 0.3.0 are kept, including your distances, so the new defaults only apply if you reset or have not changed those sliders. CHUD is licensed under the MIT license.
